# Founder Operating Console
## Version

Alpha 0.1

---

# Purpose
The Founder Operating Console is the primary workspace used to operate Odyssey.

It is designed to reduce context switching, provide clarity, surface meaningful insights, and help the founder move from planning into focused execution.
The Founder Operating Console is not a dashboard for viewing information.
It is a workspace for making decisions.

---

# Primary User

Founder
Future users:
- CEO / Managing Director
- Creative Director
- Business Development & Operations
- Finance & Investment

---

# Philosophy
The console should answer four questions immediately.

1. What deserves my attention today?
2. What decision do I need to make?
3. What opportunity should I pursue?
4. What should I build next?

# Lock In
Lock In transitions the founder from planning into execution.
When activated:

- Notifications become minimal.
- Only information relevant to the current mission remains visible.
- Studio context is fixed until intentionally changed.
- Quick Capture remains available.
